## Key Insights

### Fleet Age Trends

- **Modern Commercial Fleet**: Most commercial jets observed are relatively new:
  - Bombardier CRJ9: Average 14.0 years
  - Boeing 737-900ER: Average 7.3 years
  - Embraer E175: Average 7.7 years
  - Airbus A321neo: Average 5.8 years

- **Training Aircraft Longevity**: Cessna 172s from the 1970s still actively used for flight training:
  - C172M: Average 50.8 years old
  - C172N: Average 47.3 years old
  - These aircraft have decades of service life remaining with proper maintenance

- **Business Jet Modernization**: Newest fleet segment includes:
  - Airbus Canada BD-500 (Airbus A220): 2.1 years average
  - Multiple Gulfstream G500/G550s less than 3 years old
  - Pilatus PC-24s from 2023

- **Vintage Aircraft Preservation**:
  - 56 aircraft from the 1940s-1960s still flying
  - 2,642 observations of aircraft 55+ years old
  - Strong vintage aircraft community in the region

### Fleet Replacement Patterns

- **Aging Regional Jets**: CRJ7 (20.5 years avg) and Boeing 717 (23.7 years avg) nearing typical retirement age of 25-30 years
- **Legacy Airbus**: A320-212 fleet averaging 28.3 years, likely being replaced by A320neo family
- **New Technology**: BCS3 (Airbus A220) represents newest regional jet technology with 2.1 year average age
